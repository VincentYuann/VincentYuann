## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the confluence of classical Japanese spatial philosophy and precision modern digital craftsmanship. It is grounded in three core principles:

- **Ma (間 - Negative Space):** Empty space is treated as an active, structural element rather than absence. Expansive margins, restrained densities, and deliberate pauses allow editorial work and typography to breathe.
- **Wabi-Sabi (侘寂 - Beauty in Restraint & Imperfection):** Textured, tactile warmth replaces clinical sterile digital surfaces. Subdued tones, organic ink washes (*sumi-e*), fine shoji joinery lines, and gold repair accents (*kintsugi*) lend authenticity.
- **Shunkan (瞬間 - Intentional Tactility):** Micro-interactions mimic physical physical materials—washi translucency, red wax hanko seal impressions, and quiet, weighted transitions.

The visual style blends refined **Minimalism** with architectural **Tactile Craft**. It balances ultra-delicate 1px joinery line-work (evoking *kumiko* and *shoji* screens) with asymmetrical, harmonious bento layouts. 

### Target Audience & Emotional Tone
Designed for creative directors, architectural technologists, high-end artisans, and multidisciplinary designers. The UI evokes a sense of tranquil focus, quiet mastery, elevated culture, and unhurried confidence.

## Colors

The palette is tuned across two atmospheric states: **Asagao** (Day / Daylight Washi) and **Yozakura** (Night / Midnight Indigo).

### Color Philosophy
- **Primary — Akane / Shu-iro (朱色, `#C83C23`):** Traditional vermilion and cinnabar. Used sparingly for focal points, primary interactive states, hanko seal badges, and high-priority emphasis. In dark mode, it warms slightly to `#D3381C`.
- **Secondary — Koke / Matcha (苔色, `#4A6B5D`):** Earthy moss and stone green. Used for secondary status indicators, calm tags, and organic accents. In dark mode, it shifts toward a muted pale celadon (`#7E9F8E`).
- **Tertiary — Kohaku / Kintsugi Gold (琥珀, `#E5A93C`):** Warm lantern glow and gold-leaf lacquer. Accents high-craft micro-interactions, decorative dividers, star ratings, and night-mode ambient glows.
- **Neutral — Sumi Ink (墨, `#2B2B2B`):** Rich charcoal-soot ink used for high-contrast, non-glare text on light grounds.

### Dual Mode Specifications
- **Light Mode (Asagao):**
  - Canvas Base: `#FBF9F5` (Raw Washi Rice Paper)
  - Surface Raised / Shoji Panel: `#F4EFE6` (Refined Raw Silk / Aged Bamboo)
  - Ghost Borders & Joinery: `rgba(43, 43, 43, 0.08)`
  - Text Primary: `#2B2B2B` (Pure Sumi)
  - Text Secondary: `#6E6A63` (Granite Wash)
- **Dark Mode (Yozakura):**
  - Canvas Base: `#0F141C` (Deep Midnight Indigo)
  - Surface Raised / Shoji Panel: `#161D26` (Moonlit Slate)
  - Ghost Borders & Joinery: `rgba(244, 239, 230, 0.10)`
  - Text Primary: `#F4EFE6` (Washi White)
  - Text Secondary: `#9AA3AD` (Muted Mist)

## Typography

The typographical voice juxtaposes the classical grace of editorial serif headings (**Noto Serif**) with the crisp, modern legibility of a clean geometric sans-serif (**Plus Jakarta Sans**).

### Typographic Principles
- **Ma in Reading:** Generous line heights (`1.7x` on body copy) evoke physical book typesetting, preventing eye fatigue and emphasizing calm discovery.
- **Tategaki (Vertical Text Accents):** Selected category labels, chapter indices, and decorative bilingual phrases use vertical writing modes (`writing-mode: vertical-rl; text-orientation: upright;`) with high tracking.
- **Bilingual Framing:** Primary English headers are frequently grounded by small, understated Japanese kanji/katakana sub-labels set in `label-caps` to impart cultural context without compromising international accessibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine is modeled around traditional **Tatami grid ratios** (1:2 aspect modular balance) translated into contemporary Bento grid compositions.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Column Architecture:** Desktop layouts leverage a flexible 12-column architecture with generous section gutters (`1.5rem` / `24px`). On mobile, this reflows into a single or 2-column stacked rhythmic flow with `0.75rem` gutters.
- **Outer Canvas (Ma):** Margins are deliberately broad (`4rem` on desktop, tapering to `1.25rem` on mobile) to create a protective barrier of calm around the portfolio exhibits.
- **Asymmetric Harmony:** Large hero showcases span 8 columns, flanked by an offset 4-column column containing metadata and vertical stamp details. Empty cells are accepted and embraced as natural pauses in layout choreography.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally avoids standard blunt drop-shadows. Depth is articulated purely through **architectural layering, translucent washi frost, and delicate joinery outlines**.

- **Level 0 (Earth / Base):** Pure raw washi (`#FBF9F5` in Day) or deep indigo (`#0F141C` in Night). Unframed, foundational.
- **Level 1 (Shoji Panel):** Raised surfaces use background tints (`#F4EFE6` or `#161D26`) combined with a crisp 1px ghost boundary:
  - *Day:* `border: 1px solid rgba(43, 43, 43, 0.08);`
  - *Night:* `border: 1px solid rgba(244, 239, 230, 0.10);`
- **Level 2 (Translucent Shoji Screen):** Frosted glass panels over imagery or sumi-e wash canvases employ a subtle backdrop blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background-color: rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.82);` (Day) or `rgba(22, 29, 38, 0.85);` (Night).
- **Kintsugi Accent Lines:** High-tier interactive hover states reveal a delicate gold hairline highlight along top or left borders (`1px solid #E5A93C`) rather than lifting via heavy shadows.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`1`)** roundedness. 

- Core cards, bento modules, inputs, and architectural frames carry crisp, understated corners (`0.25rem` / `4px`), echoing precisely cut Japanese joinery (*sashimono*) and paper screens rather than bubbly consumer curves.
- Outer container frames and modal dialogs extend slightly to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- The singular exception to rectilinear geometry is the **Hanko Stamp Badge**, which utilizes either a strict square frame or a full circular stamp shape (`border-radius: 9999px`) to evoke physical cinnabar wax chops.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary (Cinnabar / Vermilion):** Solid `#C83C23` background with crisp white typography. Flat finish, no drop shadows. Subtle hover transition to dark cinnabar (`#B0311B`) with a smooth 200ms ease.
- **Secondary (Shoji Joinery):** Transparent background with 1px border (`rgba(43, 43, 43, 0.2)` in light mode, `rgba(244, 239, 230, 0.2)` in dark mode). Hover adds an inner warm tint and gold hairline border (`#E5A93C`).
- **Ghost / Text:** Understated sans-serif typography flanked by an en-dash or thin geometric arrow (`→`), transitioning with an organic horizontal slide on hover.

### 2. Hanko Badges & Seals (Signature Pattern)
- Circular or square double-bordered badges containing 1-2 kanji characters or abbreviated initials.
- Border and text rendered in Vermilion (`#C83C23`), rotated at a micro-angle (`transform: rotate(-1.5deg)`) to simulate handmade seal pressing.
- Used for project verification, featured labels, and artist signatures.

### 3. Shoji Bento Cards
- Enclosed with 1px subtle joinery borders. Internal padding adheres strictly to `space-md` (`1.25rem`) or `space-lg` (`2rem`).
- Hover states do not lift card position; instead, they activate a soft kintsugi rim glow or an internal wash transition from pure washi to a warm silk tone.

### 4. Form Controls & Inputs
- **Inputs:** Clean bottom-line underline or soft boxed inputs with `roundedness: 1` (`0.25rem`). Unfocused border is whisper-thin; focus activates an ink-black (`#2B2B2B`) or moonlit amber (`#E5A93C`) line.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist square or circular outlines featuring a vermilion fill with crisp white geometric marks when active.

### 5. Vertical Accents & Navigation (Tategaki)
- Side navigation ribbons and card category indicators run vertically along grid edges, creating an editorial layout reminiscent of classical scrolls (*emakimono*).
